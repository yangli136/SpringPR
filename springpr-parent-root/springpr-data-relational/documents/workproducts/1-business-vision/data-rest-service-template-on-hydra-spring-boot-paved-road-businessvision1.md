**Top 50 Initiative (Y/N):**

**Vision Statement:**
This project is a template project provides Data Driven Design support and dynamically exposes feature-rich REST API for underneath Data Store.

## Overview

Card on-demand APIs are designed for FinTechs and business software providers, just like you, who want to partner[^1] with American Express to provide accounts payable, expense management, and bill payment solutions for American Express Customers. Card on-demand APIs enable end-users, who are American Express Customers, to enroll their eligible American Express Card account[^2] in your software, to generate and fund on-demand virtual Cards for business payments.

## Benefits

| For your company                | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|---------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Grow your Revenue               | Approved Card-on-demand Partners[^1] can receive a revenue share on the charge volume[^4] of on-demand virtual Card transactions generated within their software.                                                       |
| Expand your target base         | Attract American Express Customers to your software by enabling enrollment of their eligible American Express Card account[^2].                                                                                         |
| Enhance the customer experience | Give your customers the option to enroll their eligible American Express Card account[^2] in your software, providing them with a wider range of payment methods and a consolidated view of their accounts in your app. |

| For your customers              | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Manage cash flow                | American Express Customers can pay suppliers using on-demand virtual Cards and take advantage of the American Express billing cycle to utilize cash on hand for their business until their Card payment is due.                      |
| Earn rewards                    | American Express Customers can earn the rewards[^3] of their eligible American Express Card account when they use it for funding on-demand virtual Cards for their business payments.                                                |
| Improve reconciliation          | Customers can have greater visibility on the billing statement for their eligible American Express accounts enrolled in your software, by seeing on-demand virtual Card transactions, helping to make reconciliation more efficient. |
| Set controls for business spend | Customers may establish specific controls for each on-demand virtual Card payment, including setting spending limits or expiration dates, and changing controls or deactivating on-demand virtual Cards at any time.                 |
| Enhance payment security        | Each recipient of an on-demand virtual Card receives a unique 15-digit number, different from the underlying American Express Card funding account number.                                                                           |

[^1]: To be eligible for American Express® Card on-Demand, Partners must apply, be approved and execute a Partnership Agreement with American Express.

[^2]: To use on-demand virtual Cards, the American Express Customer must have an American Express Small Business Card or American Express Corporate Card. American Express Small Business Employee Cards are not eligible to be enrolled in Card on-demand. Eligible American Express Cards are subject to change.

[^3]: Not all Cards are eligible to earn rewards. Terms and limitations vary by Card type.

[^4]: The revenue share earned by the Partner will be determined by the executed Partnership Agreement between Card on-demand Partners and American Express.
